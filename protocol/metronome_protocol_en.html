Privacy Policy

Welcome to "Metronome" (hereinafter referred to as the "App"). We highly value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy is intended to help you understand how we collect, use, store, and disclose your personal information.

1. Information Collection and Use

1.1 Device Information: We may collect basic information such as your device model and operating system version to optimize app performance and provide a better user experience.

1.2 App Settings: We use local storage (SharedPreferences) to save your app settings, such as tempo, sound preferences, etc. This data is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to any server.

1.3 Usage Statistics: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to understand how the app is used and improve product features.

1.4 Advertising Information: When the App displays ads, third-party advertising service providers may automatically receive or process your IP address, device information, advertising identifier, app information, network information, ad impressions and interactions, and your advertising consent status where applicable. This information may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud and abuse prevention, frequency capping, personalized or non-personalized advertising, and related purposes.

2. Permissions Used

2.1 Audio Playback: The app requires audio playback permission to provide metronome sounds. This permission is only used for playing metronome audio.

2.2 Vibration: The app may use vibration to provide haptic feedback. This permission is only used when you enable the vibration feature.

2.3 Wake Lock: The app uses wake lock permission to prevent the screen from turning off during metronome playback. This feature can be disabled in settings.

3. Information Protection and Storage

3.1 We will take reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of internet transmissions, so you use our App at your own risk.

3.2 Your personal settings and usage data are stored only locally on your device. We do not collect or store this data on remote servers.

3.3 We will retain your personal information only as long as necessary and will delete or anonymize it when it is no longer needed.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We will not sell, rent, or exchange your personal information with third parties. However, we may share your information with third parties in the following circumstances:

  • When you explicitly consent to sharing.
  • When required or permitted by law.
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, as well as the rights, property, or safety of other users.
